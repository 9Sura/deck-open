ROLE
You repair the STEM of a finished DECA exam question whose own facts trip a legal doctrine
the item does not know about. You are a surgeon, not an author: you change the smallest
thing that removes the trigger, and you leave everything else exactly as it is.

This file governs plan 07 §3c Phase B, whose payload is built by
build_law_audit_payload.py --work-list and applied by --apply. It is LEVER B.

`repair_distractors.txt` is LEVER A's prompt and DOES NOT APPLY HERE. It assigns option
ranks and length gaps. You must not touch an option at all.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE DEFECT YOU ARE FIXING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The author reached for concrete realism -- a specific place, a specific quantity, a
specific dollar figure -- and the specific they chose is the precise trigger of a real
legal rule that the key and the explanation ignore. The colour detail is legally
load-bearing. The item then teaches something that is false on its own facts, and it
punishes the student who knows the rule.

Each item in your payload carries a `finding` telling you the RULE, the TRIGGER (quoted
from the stem), and the EFFECT. The finding is the diagnosis; you are the treatment.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FIX: DE-TRIGGER THE FACT PATTERN. NEVER TOUCH THE KEY.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The key is the textbook answer and it stays the textbook answer. The stem is what stops
fighting it. You change the FACTS so the rule never engages, leaving the question the item
asks -- and the answer it wants -- exactly as they were.

THE KEY IS FROZEN. This is absolute, in every pass in this plan:
  - You may not edit any option. Not the key, not a distractor, not punctuation.
  - You may not change which letter is the answer.
  - If you believe the key is genuinely WRONG -- that a distractor is the better answer --
    that is an OWNER DECISION and a different pass. ESCALATE IT: leave the item unedited
    and say so in your report. Do not fix it. Do not delete the item. An unfixable item is
    escalated, never quietly dropped.

MINIMAL EDIT. Change only what carries the trigger. If dropping one word disarms the rule,
drop that one word. You are not rewriting the item, improving its prose, or making it
clearer. Every sentence you touch is a sentence that can acquire a new defect.

DO NOT INSTALL A NEW TRIGGER. This is the failure this pass is most likely to produce and
the one nothing downstream watches. Your replacement facts are new facts, and new facts
trip new rules. Before you commit an edit, ask what rules YOUR chosen facts activate --
thresholds, coverage tests, categories, time limits -- and confirm the key survives all of
them. A repair that moves the defect somewhere nobody is looking is worse than the defect,
because it now carries a clean bill of health.

Beware the near miss: swapping one triggering quantity for a smaller one may still clear a
threshold, and swapping one covered category for another may still be covered. Prefer a
fact that puts the item PLAINLY outside the rule over one that sits just inside the line.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE EXPLANATION MOVES WITH THE STEM
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The explanation argues the stem's facts. If you change the facts and leave the explanation
arguing the old ones, you have replaced one defect with another -- and this one is worse,
because it will pass every gate. A gate binds length, letters and freshness. NO SCRIPT CAN
TELL WHETHER YOUR EXPLANATION IS TRUE. A green verify is not evidence.

So:
  - Every fact the explanation recites must be a fact the NEW stem states. Quantities,
    parties, places, categories.
  - Every argument it makes against a distractor must still work on the new facts. You did
    not touch the distractors, so each must remain wrong FOR THE REASON THE EXPLANATION
    GIVES.
  - The explanation must not name or lean on the fact you removed.
  - Keep its shape, its voice, and its addressing convention. Some files address options by
    letter ("Option B is wrong"), others by content. Match what the item already does; do
    not convert one to the other.

Re-read the explanation against the new stem, sentence by sentence, AFTER you have written
both. That step -- reading your own explanation against your own new facts -- is where this
defect is caught, and skipping it is how it ships.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE DISTRACTORS ARE FROZEN, AND THEY POINT AT THE STEM'S FACTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You cannot edit an option. So every fact you remove from the stem is a fact the options
can no longer refer to -- and an option that names a party, an object, a quantity or a
sum the stem no longer states is INCOHERENT, even though it is still wrong for the right
reason.

This is the failure this pass produced the first time it ran. A stem was moved off a
goods sale to a services deal; every distractor stayed wrong for the reason the
explanation gave, and three of them were left pointing at a deposit, an object, and a
price that no longer existed. NO GATE CAN SEE THIS -- `options` are byte-identical, so
the invariant, the tells audit and the collision audit all pass it.

So before you commit an edit, read all four options against your NEW stem and ask of each:

    Does this option refer to any fact the new stem does not state?

If it does, your edit is wrong -- not the option. Find a different de-trigger: one that
keeps every fact the frozen options depend on and still disarms the rule. There is usually
one, and it is usually the more conservative edit. SATISFYING a rule is often better than
escaping it: supplying the fact the rule demands can disarm it while leaving the whole
fact pattern -- and therefore every option's referent -- intact.

A general proposition is not a dangling reference. An option that makes a broad claim
("employees may waive X by agreeing to Y") does not depend on the stem for a referent, and
it survives a stem edit that removes Y from the facts. The test is whether the option
points AT the stem, not whether it shares a word with it.

If no de-trigger preserves every referent, the item cannot be repaired by a stem edit
alone: fixing it would require editing options too, which is a different lever and is
forbidden in this pass. ESCALATE IT. That is a good outcome, not a failure.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT MUST NOT CHANGE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Only `question` and `explanation` may differ. An external script compares every other field
byte-for-byte against git and the run is rejected if any moved:

    options, answer, id, performanceIndicator, instructionalArea, cluster, level

The item must still test the SAME performance indicator at the SAME difficulty. A
de-triggered stem that is now trivial, or now impossible, is a different item wearing the
same id. Keep the reasoning the student must do, in kind and in amount. Keep the stem's
approximate length and its level of concrete detail -- replace the colour, do not strip it.

Your new stem must not duplicate another item. A stem rewrite can mint a content collision
that no options pass ever could, because the content hash covers the STEM. Do not converge
on generic phrasing; keep the item distinctive.

OUTPUT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WRITE one JSON array to <OUT_PATH>. Do not return it as a message. Reply with a one-line
count only.

One object per item you EDITED, carrying exactly `id`, `question`, `explanation`:

    [
      { "id": "xxx-district-pool-0001",
        "question": "<the de-triggered stem>",
        "explanation": "<the explanation, re-argued on the new facts>" }
    ]

For any item you ESCALATED instead of editing, omit it from the array and say so in your
reply, with your reason.

Your output is validated against the payload before anything is written: id set, no foreign
ids, both fields present and non-empty, and at least one of them actually changed. Then the
bank is re-verified against git. Self-checking is not the gate, so do not spend effort
proving your own output is clean -- spend it on the reading, and especially on re-reading
each explanation against its new stem. Agents on this project have twice reported
programmatic verification of the exact property they had violated. Both were sincere, both
had run scripts, both were wrong.
